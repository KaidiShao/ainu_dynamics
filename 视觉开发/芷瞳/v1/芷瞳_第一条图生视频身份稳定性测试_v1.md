# 芷瞳第一条图生视频身份稳定性测试 v1

## 目的

只验证芷瞳在极低动作下是否保持同一张脸。不验证对白、走路、转头、复杂表演或运镜。

## 输入首帧

`06_生成结果/芷瞳_标准半侧脸_原图保脸清理候选_v6.png`

## 建议参数

- 模式：image-to-video
- 时长：5—6 秒
- 画幅：保持输入图竖幅，不扩图
- 帧率：24 fps（若平台可选）
- 动作强度：低
- 镜头运动：关闭／固定机位
- 身份或参考图强度：高（若平台可选）
- 固定随机种子：开启（若平台可选）
- 不启用口型、对白、自动运镜或镜头延展

## 主提示词（英文）

> Locked camera, a single continuous close portrait. The same adult East Asian woman remains almost perfectly still in the same 55—65 degree half-profile, with her exact face, head pose, expression, warm outdoor light, off-white clothing and green background preserved from the input image. She takes one subtle natural breath, completes one soft unhurried blink, then shifts only her eyes a few degrees toward the unseen space in front of her. A very faint breeze moves only the loose tips of her hair. Her lips remain closed and still. Quiet, observant and self-contained; realistic micro-movement, real human skin texture, no performance for the camera.

## 负面约束（英文）

> Do not change her identity, facial proportions, age, makeup, skin tone, hairstyle, clothing, background, lighting or head angle. No speaking, lip movement, smile, frown, crying, eyebrow acting, head turn, chin lift, shoulder movement, hand entering frame, body turn, walking, camera pan, zoom, dolly, orbit, rack focus, scene transition, background motion, face morphing, eye enlargement, jaw narrowing, nose shortening, beauty filter, skin smoothing, flicker, warping, duplicate features or extra people.

## 时间节奏

- 0.0—1.5 秒：保持静止，仅有极轻微呼吸。
- 1.5—2.5 秒：一次完整、自然、偏慢的眨眼。
- 2.5—4.2 秒：只移动眼球，目光向画外轻移几度；头部不动。
- 4.2—6.0 秒：保持新目光落点，发梢有极小幅度风动。

## 验收标准

通过必须同时满足：

- 第一帧、中段与末帧仍能认作同一个芷瞳；
- 鼻尖、唇形、下颌宽度、眼距和发际线没有漂移；
- 眨眼没有造成双眼大小永久改变；
- 目光移动没有带动整张脸转向；
- 嘴唇全程闭合且没有自动说话；
- 背景与衣服没有呼吸、融化、闪烁或变形；
- 镜头没有自行推近、横移或环绕。

若失败，下一轮只删减一个动作：优先保留呼吸与眨眼，暂时移除目光移动和风动。
